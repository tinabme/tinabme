# Quick and easy sugar cookies

A soft, easy, kid-friendly sugar cookie that is lower in sugar and perfect for decorating or enjoying as-is.

**Difficulty level (1-5 stars) \*1 being easy, 5 being difficult\*:**

2 stars

**Ingredients + Measurements:**

## Cookies

- 1 cup all-purpose flour  
- 2 tablespoons sugar  
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder  
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter (softened at room temperature)  
- 2 tablespoons milk  
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract  

## Easy Icing (optional)

- 1/4 cup powdered sugar  
- 2-3 teaspoons milk  
- 1-2 drops food coloring (optional)  

## Instructions

- Gather all your cookie ingredients into a big bowl and gently mix until everything comes together in a soft dough. If the dough feels a bit dry or crumbly, add a spoonful more milk and keep kneading.
- Shape the dough into a round disc and wrap it in plastic. Pop it in the fridge while you let the oven warm up.
- Set your oven to 375°F (190°C) and lay out two baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Roll out your dough on a lightly floured surface, until it’s about ¼-inch thick.
- Use your favorite cookie cutters to cut out shapes, and carefully place them onto the baking sheets. If at any point the dough feels tricky to move, tuck it back into the fridge for a few minutes to firm up. (patience makes perfect cookies!)
- Bake your cookies for 7–9 minutes, just until the edges turn a soft golden color. Let them rest on the baking sheet or wire rack, they need a little time to cool off before you add any icing.

### Make the Icing

- In a small bowl, stir together the icing ingredients. You’re looking for a texture that spreads gently and stays in place.
- Spread the icing over the cooled cookies using a butter knife.

### Tips/Tricks
- You can replcae the milk with cream for a richer taste.
- If your icing is too runny, just add a bit more powdered sugar. If it’s a little thick, a drop or two of milk or cream
