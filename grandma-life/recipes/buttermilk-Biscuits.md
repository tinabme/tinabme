## Melt in your mouth Buttermilk Biscuits ##

Fluffy, easy, buttery, mouthwatering biscuits you can enjoy with any meal.

<img width="617" alt="4 stacked buttermilk-biscuits" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4c332a9c-5635-4997-a137-b1438effb4c1">


**Difficulty level (1-5 stars) \*1 being easy, 5 being difficult\*:** 

2 stars

**Ingredients + Measurements:**

-	2 cups self-raising flour (sifted)
-	1 stick of real butter (very cold)
-	1 cup of buttermilk (very cold)

**Directions:**
- Preheat your oven to 450 degrees.
- Shred the stick of butter using a cheese grater and place it in the freezer while the oven heats up.
- Spoon flour into the bowl with the shredded butter and gently mix so the butter pieces are coated with flour.
- Pour in the cold buttermilk and stir just until a dough forms.
- Sprinkle some flour on the counter, then dump the dough onto it.
- Flour your hands and gently pat and fold the dough several times to create layers and flatten it to about 1 inch thick. It should be a bit sticky; no rolling pin needed. (Work quickly to keep the dough from warming)
- Cut out the biscuits and place them in a greased skillet or pie pan so their sides are touching.
- Bake for 12-16 minutes, or until they are cooked through.


**Tips/Tricks:**
- Keep the dough cold and slightly sticky.
- Don’t twist the cutter when cutting the biscuits to avoid sealing the sides.
- Place the biscuits in the pan with their sides touching for better results.
- Make sure the oven is fully preheated before baking. You can chill the biscuits in the fridge while waiting.
- If the tops get too dark, cover them with a piece of foil to finish cooking.
